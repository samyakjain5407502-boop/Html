Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Jainzee Food Processing Industries ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

  • Name, phone number, and email address provided during registration or checkout.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is stored securely. Passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text.
