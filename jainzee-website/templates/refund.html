Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Cancellation

  • Orders can be cancelled before they are dispatched. Use the Cancel option on your order or contact us.
  • Once dispatched, an order cannot be cancelled but may be returned as per the return terms below.

Returns

  • Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for damaged, defective, or wrong items.
  • Products must be unopened and in their original packaging (except for damaged-item claims).

Refunds

  • Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • Shipping charges are refunded only if the return is due to our error.

How to Request

Contact us at +91 98260 00000 with your order ID, photos of the item (if damaged), and your request. Our team will respond within 24 hours.
