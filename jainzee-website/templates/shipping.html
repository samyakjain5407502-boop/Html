Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Dispatch Time

Orders are typically dispatched within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed the next business day.

Delivery Time

  • Indore and nearby areas: 1–3 business days.
  • Rest of India: 3–7 business days.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, are shown clearly at checkout before you place your order. Free shipping may be offered on qualifying orders.

Packaging

All products are packed in food-grade, sealed packaging to ensure freshness and hygiene during transit.

Delivery Issues

If your order is delayed or damaged in transit, please contact us at +91 98260 00000 with your order ID and we will resolve it promptly.
